1. Who we are

This policy explains how the Status Downloader app for Android (package com.composerastudio.statusdownloader, “the app”) handles information. The app is developed and published by Compose R.A Studio (“we”, “us”), which is responsible for the processing described here.

The app is free to download. Its free version shows ads, and an optional paid Premium upgrade removes them.

Status Downloader is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc.

2. Information that stays on your device

The app has no user accounts, and we do not run servers that receive your content. Everything in this section is processed only on your device and is never sent to us.

Status files and folder access

To show the statuses you have already viewed in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, the app needs access to the folder where WhatsApp keeps them on your device.

  • On Android 11 and later, you choose that folder yourself in Android’s system folder picker. The app can open only that folder, and you can revoke access at any time in Settings → Folders.
  • On Android 10 and earlier, the app asks for the storage permission, which it requests only on those versions.

The app does not access your contacts, chats, messages, call history or location.

Saved photos and videos

When you save a status, or when auto-save is on, the app copies it to your gallery: images to Pictures/StatusDownloader and videos to Movies/StatusDownloader. These copies are ordinary files on your device. They stay there until you delete them, even if you uninstall the app.

Background checks

If you turn on Notifications or Auto-save in Settings → Automation, the app checks the statuses folder from time to time (about every 15 minutes, as scheduled by Android) to tell you about new statuses or to save them. This check runs entirely on your device, and notifications are created locally, without any push-notification service.

Sharing and reposting

When you share or repost a status, the app makes a temporary copy in its private storage and hands it to the app you choose. From then on, that app’s own privacy policy applies. Temporary copies are deleted the next time the app starts.

Settings and preferences

The app stores on your device your theme, language and grid preferences; the folder access you granted; your favourites; which statuses you have already been notified about (identified by file name and size); your tutorial progress; counters that limit how often ads appear; and whether Premium is active, so that a paying user never sees an ad while Google Play is still connecting.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include these settings in your device backup. That backup is managed by Google under your account settings.

You can delete all of this at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Status Downloader → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising, consent and your choices

The free version shows full-screen ads only after you finish an action, such as saving a status, and never when the app opens.

Europe (EEA), United Kingdom and Switzerland

When you open the app, a consent message from Google’s certified consent platform explains how your data is used for advertising and lets you accept, refuse or choose specific purposes and partners. No ads are requested until you have made a choice. If you do not consent to personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads, as your choices allow.

United States

Some US state privacy laws treat sharing data with advertising partners for targeted advertising as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money. If you live in a state where these laws apply, you can opt out of this sale or sharing for targeted advertising with the option described below.

Changing your choices

Where these rules apply, the app shows Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options. Use it at any time to review or withdraw your consent, or to opt out. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it.

Wherever you are, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on most devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads), and manage how Google personalises ads in My Ad Center.

Premium

If you have Premium, the app shows no ads, and when it opens it starts neither the advertising service nor the consent message.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Folder access or storage (storage only on Android 10 and earlier): to read status files and save the copies you ask for.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you about new statuses and about files saved automatically.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads, consent messages and Premium purchases, and to send usage statistics and crash reports.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and restore Premium.
  • Advertising ID, Android ad services and install referrer: used by AdMob and Firebase as described in section 3.
  • Background work (wake lock and Android’s job scheduler): so that Android can run the optional status check reliably.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent: storing and reading information on your device for advertising, and personalised advertising.
  • Legitimate interests: showing ads that fund the free version within the limits of your choices; preventing fraud; keeping the app stable and secure through crash reports; understanding how the app is used in aggregate; and answering your messages.
  • Performance of a contract: processing and restoring your Premium purchase.

7. How long information is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete it, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Saved photos and videos stay in your gallery until you delete them.
  • Consent choices: on your device until you change them or clear the app’s data. Google may ask you again when required, for example when its list of ad partners changes.
  • Crash reports: 90 days (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Usage statistics: user-level data is kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for no more than 14 months.
  • Advertising data: according to Google’s policies.
  • Support emails: only as long as needed to resolve your request and follow up on it.

8. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information for money. Apart from the Google services described above, which process it to provide their services, we do not share it with anyone, unless we are required to by law.

9. International transfers

10. Security

Information sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The app opens only the folder you grant, and keeps temporary copies in its private storage, deleting them regularly. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, to withdraw your consent at any time, and to opt out of targeted advertising or of the sale or sharing of personal information. We will never treat you differently for exercising these rights.

Because the app has no accounts and we keep no data on servers of our own, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device:

  • Ad consent and opt-out: Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • Data on your device: clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.

For anything else, including requests about data that Google processes for the app, write to composerastudio@gmail.com. We will answer within the time limits set by law (generally one month, or 45 days for requests under US state laws). If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
